Grand Vision Heavy Industry Limited ("we", "us", "our", or the "Company"), a company registered in Hong Kong SAR, is the operator of the mobile mini-game "Funburst Island" (the "Game") offered on the TikTok platform. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you play the Game, how we use it, and the rights you have.

1. Information We Collect

When you play the Game through the TikTok platform, the following categories of information may be collected:

  • Platform account information provided by TikTok (such as a user identifier and public display name) when you launch the Game or use ranking and sharing features;
  • Gameplay data, including level progress, scores, in-game currency, item inventory, and settings, stored to enable saving and restoring your game;
  • Device and technical data, such as device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and network type, collected automatically to keep the Game running correctly;

We do not collect your phone number, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone recordings through the Game. We do not access your TikTok friends list.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate the Game, save your progress, and restore your saved data;

  • To credit rewards when you watch rewarded video advertisements;
  • To maintain the stability, security, and performance of the Game;
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

3. Third-Party Services

The Game runs on, and relies on, the following third-party services, each processing data under its own privacy policy:

4. Data Storage and Security

Gameplay data is stored on servers located in regions approved by the TikTok platform, protected by reasonable technical and organizational measures including encrypted transmission. We retain gameplay data only for as long as necessary to provide the Game's features or as required by law.

5. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
